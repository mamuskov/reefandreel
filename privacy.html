Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Reef & Reel is a fishing and aquarium game for iPhone, published by Mahmut Akyol ("we"). This page explains what happens to your data when you play.

In short

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device. We do not receive them.
  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data ourselves.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your catches, aquarium, boat, money, settings (language, sound, vibration) and similar game progress are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes them, and Settings in the game can erase everything at any time.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows short ads at natural breaks and rewarded ads you choose to watch. They are provided by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device and connection information, such as device model, operating system version, language, IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • ad activity in the app, such as which ads were shown and tapped;
  • an advertising identifier. On iPhone, Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is only available if you allow tracking when the game asks. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can review or change that choice at any time in the game's Settings under Ad privacy options.

Google handles this data under its own policies: the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting services are built into the game.
  • The game does not ask for your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • There are no in-app purchases at this time.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your choices

  • Erase your game data in the game's Settings, or by deleting the app.
  • Turn tracking off in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Change your ad consent in the game's Settings under Ad privacy options (EEA, UK and Switzerland).

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new version and its date.

Contact
